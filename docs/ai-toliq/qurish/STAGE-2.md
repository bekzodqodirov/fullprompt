### 4.3 P2 «Ikki bosqich» — the two-phase turn (owns the top of `handleInbound`, `worthy.ts`, the readings part of `prompt.ts`)

A 150-second reader cannot sit inside a run (`run.ts:116` wall 90 s, `:267` 1 500 tokens a round, 60 s a call). So, in `handleInbound` (`run.ts:447`) after the typing wait (`:469`) and BEFORE `draftWorthy` (`:474`):

1. `material = await burstMaterial(ctx)` — the burst's kept attachments with their reading rows; a kept file with no row gets one (`queued`) and its job. At most 6 files a burst are read; the rest are `skipped` / `too_many` and named.
2. **Any reading still in flight** and the job has waited less than 30 s: `reschedule(job, attempt, 6, deps, 'reading')` (`run.ts:127` — the SAME ask, its attempt carried). Most Excel files and many photos land inside this wait: ONE turn, no holding line.
3. **Still in flight after the cap:** stamp `wake_anchor_tg_message_id` on the in-flight rows, write the ledger row `refused` / `reading_wait` (`runLedgerRow`, `run.ts:134` — not counted by any cap, and it stops the sweep's gap pass re-asking), call ⟨1b⟩'s `queueHoldingLine` with the reading wording (§6.3), return. No model call.
